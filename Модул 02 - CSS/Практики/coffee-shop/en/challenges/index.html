Little Fern Coffee Co. · CSS exercise · Български

5 challenges, one coffee shop

Challenge 1

Navigation bar

  • flex
  • justify-content
  • gap

Goal: Logo on the left, links on the right, on one centred row.

Looks like: a slim, single-row green header.

Challenge 2

Menu grid

  • grid
  • fr
  • gap

Goal: Show the 6 menu cards in 3 columns and the Visit boxes in 2.

Looks like: two rows of equal cards, prices lined up at the bottom.

Challenge 3

Hover effects

  • :hover
  • transition
  • transform

Goal: Buttons, cards and nav links react smoothly to the mouse.

Looks like: cards lift with a shadow, links get a yellow underline.

Challenge 4

Positioning

  • relative
  • absolute
  • sticky

Goal: Add “Bestseller” badges to two cards and make the header sticky.

Looks like: pill badges on card corners, header stays on top while scrolling.

Challenge 5

Responsive

  • @media
  • clamp()
  • mobile first

Goal: 1 menu column on phones, 2 on tablets, 3 on desktop.

Looks like: the layout adapts as you resize. No sideways scrolling.
