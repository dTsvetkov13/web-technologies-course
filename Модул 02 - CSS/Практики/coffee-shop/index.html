Our beans

We buy green coffee directly from three small farms in Colombia, Ethiopia and Guatemala. Then we roast it ourselves, in small 12 kg batches, so every bag you take home is less than a week old.

Not sure what you like? Tell the barista how you take your coffee and we’ll pour you a free tasting sample.
